Frequently Asked Questions

Understand the opportunity before you commit.

Clear answers about experience, costs, licensing, time commitment, compensation, support, and the difference between the two partnership paths.

What does it cost to get started?

For the licensed business-owner path, the initial registration is $199. State licensing, pre-licensing education, background, and related fees vary by state. E&O professional insurance is approximately $35 per month. Current costs should be confirmed before you register.

Do I need prior financial-services experience?

No. Prior experience can help, but it is not required. Regulated activities require the appropriate state licensing, carrier appointments, training, and compliance before you can make recommendations or conduct business.

Can I start part-time?

Yes. Many professionals begin alongside an existing career or business. Your activity level should fit your availability, licensing status, and client-service responsibilities.

What services can I learn to provide?

Depending on licensing, carrier appointments, and approved product access, the work may include life insurance, living benefits, final expense, retirement and rollover education, Fixed Indexed Annuities, and coordinated legacy conversations.

How do I earn income?

Licensed professionals may earn compensation on eligible business written according to carrier contracts, licensing, and applicable program terms. Compensation is not guaranteed. Referral arrangements, where permitted, are governed by applicable law and program rules.

Is this a job or an independent business path?

The business-owner path is designed around building an independent financial services practice rather than traditional salaried employment. Your exact agreements, responsibilities, and compensation structure should be reviewed before you enroll.

What training and support are provided?

The model includes licensing guidance, training, mentorship, field support, product education, and business-development structure. The level and type of support can depend on your stage and licensing status.

What is the referral-partner path?

It is for relationship-driven professionals who want to connect people to Watts Unified Solutions without building a full financial services practice. Referral activity and any compensation, if applicable, must follow state law, program terms, and licensing rules.

Do I have to recruit people?

No. You can focus on serving clients and building your own book of business. Developing other qualified professionals can be one way to grow, but it should not replace client service or professional production.

What is the best first step?

Review the opportunity and schedule a private conversation. Ask your questions, confirm current costs and licensing requirements, and decide whether the model fits your goals before registering.

Still have a question?

Bring it to a private opportunity conversation. The goal is clarity before commitment.
