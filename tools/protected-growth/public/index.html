Protected

A protected growth strategy links your gains to market performance while holding a guaranteed 0% floor underneath your principal. When markets rise, your account has the opportunity to grow. When markets fall, your balance holds steady instead of falling with it.

The Core Problem

A market crash late in life can undo decades of saving.

The years right before and after retirement are the most vulnerable to market drops. A steep decline at the wrong time, combined with ongoing withdrawals, can permanently shrink how long your money lasts — even if the market eventually recovers.

Direct Market Exposure

−20%

A downturn like this means your account must gain roughly 25% just to return to where it started, all while you may still be withdrawing income.

Protected Growth Strategy

0% Floor

The same downturn has no effect on your principal or prior gains. Your balance simply holds where it was and waits for the next up cycle.

How It Works

Participate in the upside. Sit out the downside.

Instead of owning the market directly, your account value is credited based on the performance of a market index over a set period. Gains up to a cap or participation rate are added to your account and locked in. If the index is negative for that period, your account is simply credited 0% — no losses, no exceptions.
